Other Worlds

A speculative thriller
“Hi, my name is Rally, and this story starts with me waking up naked in the backseat of a stranger’s car.”

Rally enters other worlds, coded spaces where the real and the unreal meet, and closes them down before anything can go in or leak out. It’s freelance work for an agency no one has heard of.

Then a killer known only as X starts bringing a mysterious paint out of those worlds and into ours, a paint that incinerates its victims. As the bodies pile up and the people she loves get caught in the fire, Rally sets out to stop him.

She’s ready to be his executioner. She’s just not sure she should be his judge.

About

After graduating with a computer science degree, I spent my spare time writing and directing indie films. Today I’m a systems engineer for a consortium of colleges in Southern California. I live with my wife and two young kids. Most of this book was written after they fell asleep.
